# DoaDog QA Checklist

## Visual

- Light theme is default.
- Dark theme does not use pure black as the dominant surface.
- Logo is not distorted.
- Generated illustrations render on Home, Register Dog, Help and Profile.
- Dog cards are photo-first and readable.
- Empty states remain simple and action-oriented.

## Navigation

- Tabs: Início, Cadastrar, Ajuda, Perfil.
- Settings opens from Perfil.
- Dog detail sticky action bar does not collide with the bottom tab.
- Scroll views leave enough bottom padding.

## Functional Smoke

- App opens.
- Create dog flow saves a dog locally.
- Saved dog appears on Home.
- Dog detail opens.
- Theme switches between Sistema, Claro and Escuro.
- Help request form remains local and does not promise payment processing.

## Accessibility

- Buttons have readable labels.
- Main controls are at least 44/48px visually.
- Long text does not overlap cards or buttons.
- Contact text can be selected where useful.
